AngliaAI website — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript
==================================================

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click index.html. It opens in any modern browser; no server, build step or internet connection is needed.

FILES
  index.html, platform.html, assurance.html, who-we-serve.html, why-angliaai.html,
  founder.html, roadmap.html, insights.html, contact.html      the nine pages
  privacy.html, terms.html, data-protection.html                 placeholder legal pages
  css/style.css      all styles (one file)
  js/main.js         all interactions (menu, architecture diagram, product tabs, form, reveals)
  fonts/             Geist Sans and Geist Mono
  images/            founder photo and site icon

TO PUBLISH
  Upload the whole folder, keeping the structure, to any web host.

STILL TO REPLACE BEFORE LAUNCH
  - Contact email, LinkedIn link and company registration details (footer and contact.html)
  - Founder professional-profile link, dates and exact role titles (founder.html)
  - A higher-resolution founder photo (images/founder-farjana-khan.jpg is 140px)
  - The domain "https://www.example.com" in each page's <head>
  - Legal page text
  - The contact form is not connected to anything yet; see section 5 of js/main.js

NOTE ON EDITING
  Text can be edited directly in the .html files. The header and footer are repeated in every page,
  so a navigation change must be made in each file. css/style.css was generated from the Next.js
  version of the site (utility classes); for larger design changes, edit that project and re-export.
