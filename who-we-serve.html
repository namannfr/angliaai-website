Who we serve

Designed for organisations that own or operate an English standard.

AngliaAI is not a test sold to candidates. It is proposed as infrastructure for the organisations that set the standard and answer for the result.

Audiences

Designed for

Universities & Pathway Providers

  • Pre-sessional programmes
  • Entry diagnostics
  • Progress assessment
  • Exit assessment
  • In-house English assessment

The planned starting point: programmes that already run their own speaking assessment.

Designed for

Awarding Bodies

  • Examiner standardisation
  • Second marking
  • Consistency monitoring
  • Assessment assurance

Approached through examiner standardisation, with the engine working behind human examiners.

Designed for

Professional Regulators

  • High-stakes professional assessment
  • Evidence and auditability
  • Controlled deployment

A later stage, dependent on evidence from earlier deployments.

Designed for

Government & Public Assessment

  • Large-scale assessment infrastructure
  • Secure digital testing
  • Defensible scoring

A long-term direction. Not part of the base plan.

These are intended audiences. AngliaAI does not yet have customers in any of these categories.

Who is involved

Five roles touch one assessment. Each needs something different.

Programme Director

ConcernCost, capacity, scheduling and delivery

Results out on time, within budget, with nothing embarrassing in them.

Quality Officer

ConcernReliability, fairness, evidence and defensibility

To answer the academic board without calling the vendor.

Certified Examiner

ConcernEfficient adjudication and clear evidence

A short, clearly scoped queue rather than a full marking load.

Candidate

ConcernTransparency and understanding of the assessment

To understand how they were judged, and how to contest it.

Registrar / Auditor

ConcernReconstruction and audit trail

To inspect a historic decision and export the evidence independently.

Market entry

We start behind the examiner, not in place of the examiner.

The first product is assurance and examiner standardisation: second marking, drift detection and consistency reporting for the assessment you already run. It does not ask an institution to replace human assessment with AI on day one.

  1. Wave 1Starting point

    UK university pre-sessional and pathway programmes

    Departmental scale. Entered with assurance and examiner standardisation.

  2. Wave 2

    University admissions and in-house English testing

    Opens on an evidence pack produced in Wave 1.

  3. Wave 3

    Awarding bodies and examiner networks

    Opens on a published track record, entered through examiner standardisation.

  4. Wave 4

    Professional regulators and government testing

    Requires referenceable regulated deployments. Not in the base plan.

Planned sequence. Each wave depends on evidence produced in the one before it.
