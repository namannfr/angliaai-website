Development stage

What is evidenced, what is proposed, and what is still to be shown.

AngliaAI is an early-stage venture. We have not yet run a pilot, tested pricing, produced a comparability study or signed a letter of intent. We would rather say so plainly.

Evidenced

Supported by evidence

  • Market and regulatory developments, cited to published sources
  • Peer-reviewed research on marking reliability and AI speaking assessment
  • The founder’s professional experience
Proposed

What AngliaAI proposes to build

  • The five-layer architecture and confidence gate
  • The evidence pack and assurance reporting
  • The roadmap releases on this page
To be validated

What remains to be validated

  • Pilot results and customer demand
  • Pricing
  • Comparability with human examiners and technical performance

Evidence plan

How each claim becomes evidence.

For each differentiator, the first evidence that would substantiate it.

Assurance as the product
A completed evidence pack from a paid pilot, with agreement statistics against certified examiners on a client’s own rubric.
Client-owned standard
A signed pilot agreement containing the client-ownership terms.
Interaction, not read-aloud
A published construct model, plus measured performance on spontaneous and controlled speech.
Compounding ground truth
A first set of examiner-adjudicated cases captured under a standardisation engagement.

Deliberately out of scope

  • No candidate-facing test brand
  • No learning or practice product, which would compromise assessment validity
  • No invigilation or identity-verification business
