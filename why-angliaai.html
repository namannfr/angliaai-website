Why AngliaAI

To make high-stakes spoken-English assessment more consistent, transparent and defensible.

And to do so without removing the human expertise that makes assessment credible.

Three principles

Evidence over assertion

The system should show why a result can be trusted.

Human oversight where it matters

Automation should not mean removing meaningful human judgement.

Transparency by design

Limitations, confidence and adverse findings should not be hidden.

Market landscape

A different layer of the assessment stack.

AngliaAI is designed as neutral assessment infrastructure, not as another branded candidate-facing test. Our research groups the market into six categories.

  1. Group 1

    Branded high-stakes tests

    Each offers its own test under its own standard and brand.

  2. Group 2

    White-label and institution-run tests

    Packaged tests delivered under an institution’s name. The closest category to AngliaAI.

  3. Group 3

    Scoring engines and APIs

    Automated scoring components that other products build on.

  4. Group 4

    AI oral-examination tools

    Tools for spoken assessment in higher education. They show institutional appetite.

  5. Group 5

    Human examiner networks

    In-house marking teams: the established method, and a permanent part of our design.

  6. Group 6

    General-purpose AI

    Conversational capability without calibration to a rubric, comparability evidence or an audit trail.

Five differentiators

What we intend to be different.

  1. 01

    Assurance as the product

    Evidence is part of the commercial proposition. Each deployment is designed to produce a validity pack: agreement with certified examiners, reliability, distribution alignment and fairness, refreshed each cycle.

  2. 02

    Client-owned standard

    The organisation keeps its own standard, calibration profile and candidate data. AngliaAI provides the engine. Neutrality is intended to be contractual.

  3. 03

    Interaction, not read-aloud

    The platform is designed around genuine two-way spoken interaction: topic development, repair, listener responsiveness and turn management, scored at discourse level.

  4. 04

    Examiner-grade standardisation

    Human examiners remain part of the system. The first product places the engine behind examiners for second marking, drift detection and consistency reporting.

  5. 05

    Compounding ground truth

    Low-confidence cases are reviewed by certified examiners, creating adjudicated reference data that can improve future calibration.

Proposed product architecture. Capabilities are subject to validation and roadmap delivery.

What we do not claim

Credibility depends as much on what is left unsaid.

Not a technology claim

We do not assert superior speech recognition or a better model. Automated scoring is becoming widely available.

Not an absence-of-competition claim

The sector is contested and well funded. Our argument rests on neutrality and assurance capability.

Not yet proven

No pilot, benchmark or comparability study exists. Every differentiator on this page is a designed capability.
