Her career has centred on helping different examiners apply one standard.

Farjana Khan is the founder of AngliaAI, bringing extensive international experience in English-language assessment, examiner training, academic leadership and education quality assurance. Her career spans senior roles with Anglia Examinations, Cambridge Assessment International and Pearson BTEC, alongside her work as an IELTS Examiner and Examiner-Trainer for the British Council across Bangladesh, Nepal and Pakistan.

With postgraduate qualifications in English and English Language Teaching, CELTA from the University of Cambridge, CITE from Chichester College and specialist Business English training from the University of South Carolina, she combines deep assessment knowledge with international education experience.

Why this problem?

Farjana’s professional experience has focused on a challenge at the heart of high-stakes assessment: ensuring that different examiners apply standards consistently, fairly and reliably.

This expertise directly informs AngliaAI’s mission to develop assessment infrastructure that can operate under an institution’s own English-language standards while providing evidence of reliability, fairness, calibration and auditability.

At AngliaAI, Farjana leads the assessment-science, calibration, quality and industry-access dimensions of the venture, working alongside technical specialists to translate examiner expertise into trusted AI-powered assessment technology.
