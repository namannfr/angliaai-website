The themes we are writing about, and the published sources cited across this site.

Themes

What we are working on.

Articles on these themes are in preparation. Summaries are shown here; full pieces will be linked as they are published.

  • In preparation

    Why scoring is easier than assuring a score

    Producing a number is a solved problem in many settings. Detecting when the number is wrong is the part a regulator examined in 2026.[1]

  • In preparation

    Human examiner consistency

    One peer-reviewed study of an institutional speaking test attributed 36% of score variation to candidates, 43% to disagreement between raters and 21% to raters disagreeing with themselves.[2]

  • In preparation

    The challenge of assessing spontaneous spoken interaction

    Published evaluation of an AI speaking-assessment tool reports weaker performance on spontaneous speech than on controlled tasks.[3]

  • In preparation

    What assessment evidence should look like

    Agreement, reliability, distribution alignment and fairness: what a quality committee needs in order to approve results without asking a vendor to vouch for them.

  • In preparation

    AI and fairness in spoken-English assessment

    Why cohort-level findings, including adverse ones, belong in front of the client rather than behind a marketing claim.

  • In preparation

    Examiner standardisation

    The discipline by which many human judges are brought to one standard, and what it means to encode it.[2]

  • In preparation

    Designing auditable assessment systems

    Sealed records, version pinning and superseding rather than editing: how a decision can be reconstructed years later.[8]
